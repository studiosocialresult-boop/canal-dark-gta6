# YouTube Shorts: recommended daily posting volume for a "cortes" (clips) channel in the gaming niche (state as of Nov 2026)

Source-type labels used below: **[OFFICIAL]** = YouTube/Google; **[DATA]** = third-party dataset/study; **[VENDOR/BLOG]** = tool vendor or SEO blog (often selling clipping/scheduling tools); **[ANECDOTAL]** = creator/forum opinion. **(OLDER)** = published before 2024.

## 1. What does YouTube officially say about Shorts upload frequency?

### Takeaway
YouTube has never published a recommended number of Shorts per day. The most direct official statement (Shorts product lead Todd Sherman with Creator Liaison Rene Ritchie, Aug 2023, OLDER) is that posting more Shorts does not by itself expand or reduce reach, but posting many lower-quality clips can hurt, and deleting/re-uploading risks being treated as spam. Newer official material (2025–2026) is about limits, notifications, view counting and the "inauthentic content" monetization policy, not about a target cadence.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, OLDER — Aug 24, 2023] In a Creator Insider/YouTube interview, Shorts Product Lead Todd Sherman said the algorithm does not dictate "expanded or reduced reach if you post more Shorts" (reporter's paraphrase), but warned about negative impacts "if you generated a bunch of relatively lower quality videos and then posted those"; effort is "probably better spent in just making a better video and fewer of them." — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-advice-for-shorts-creators-hashtags-algorithm/691835/)
- [OFFICIAL, OLDER — Aug 2023] On deleting and re-uploading Shorts that underperformed, Sherman: "I would not advise that… there's also a risk that it gets seen as spam in our systems." — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-advice-for-shorts-creators-hashtags-algorithm/691835/)
- [OFFICIAL, OLDER — Aug 2023] Secondary coverage of the same interview headlined it as "Posting 10 Videos A Day Doesn't Affect Your Performance"; the "10 a day" figure appears to be the headline writer's example, not a verbatim Sherman quote (the Benzinga page returned 403, could not verify body text). Coverage also says Sherman stressed responding quickly matters more than a fixed schedule, except for news content. — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/34020532); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/388588/youtube-shorts-product-lead-offers-insights-for-cr.html)
- [Third-party summary of official interview, OLDER — Sep 4, 2023] Buffer's write-up of the Ritchie/Sherman conversation states "There's no magic number for how many Shorts you should post daily or weekly" and that time of day doesn't impact performance — but these are the Buffer author's statements, not direct quotes from YouTube staff. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [OFFICIAL — current Help page] "Viewers can get a maximum of 3 new video notifications from each channel in a 24-hour period." and "If you publish more than 3 videos in a short time period, we may temporarily stop sending notifications for 24 hours" (applies even if all but one video is set not to notify). — [YouTube Help: Fix subscriber notification problems](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7389684?hl=en-GB)
- [OFFICIAL — July 2025] YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" YPP policy to "inauthentic content," effective July 15, 2025; it targets mass-produced/repetitive content and applies at the channel level (affects monetization, not a removal from the platform). Rene Ritchie described it as a minor update with "no impact" on most creators unless content relies heavily on duplication/mass production. Coverage lists examples such as template-based videos with only minor differences. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-clarifies-monetization-update-inauthentic-repeated-content/752892/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/); [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660)
- [OFFICIAL — API revision history] Shorts view counting changed March 31, 2025: views count each time a Short "starts to play or replay, with no minimum watch time requirement"; on Aug 27, 2026 YouTube aligned public view counting across formats ("count public views the moment a video begins to play"), while monetization still uses "Engaged Shorts views." — [YouTube Data API revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/revision_history)

### Inferences
- The only "official" frequency guidance is qualitative: volume itself is neutral; low-quality volume and delete/re-upload tactics are the risk. There is no official ceiling short of the upload limits in section 3.
- For a channel that publishes 10–20 clips/day, the officially documented side effect is concrete: subscribers get at most 3 upload notifications per 24h, and bursts of >3 uploads can pause notifications for 24h. Spacing uploads matters more than total count for the notification channel.
- For a "cortes" channel that also generates *variations* of the same clip, the July 2025 inauthentic-content policy is the main official risk to monetization (template-based videos with minor differences are a named example). Posting several near-identical variations on the same YouTube channel is the riskiest pattern.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 YouTube/Creator Insider statement giving a Shorts-per-day number was found. No verbatim 2025–2026 Rene Ritchie statement on "posting too much" was found (searches surfaced only the 2023 Sherman interview and the 2025 inauthentic-content remarks).
- YouTube Creator Academy/Help pages do not state a Shorts consistency number (none found).

## 2. Does posting many Shorts per day cannibalize views between them or hurt the channel?

### Takeaway
No official source says Shorts cannibalize each other; YouTube's stance is that frequency isn't a ranking factor but low-quality output can hurt. The best 2026 dataset (vendor, associational) finds per-video median views roughly flat from 1 to 4+ Shorts/day within the same channel, while total daily views rise almost linearly — i.e., no clear cannibalization, but also no per-video boost. An unconfirmed late-2025 practitioner theory says Shorts older than ~30 days lose impressions, which would favor a steady flow of new uploads.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, OLDER — 2023] Posting more Shorts doesn't inherently change reach; many lower-quality clips can have negative impact. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-advice-for-shorts-creators-hashtags-algorithm/691835/)
- [DATA/VENDOR — Oct 29, 2026, OverseerOS, 16,815 Shorts, views observed 7–30 days after posting] Median views per Short by number of Shorts the channel posted that UTC day (narrow 14–22-day window): 1/day = 28,903; 2/day = 21,422; 3/day = 28,768; 4+/day = 15,076. Within-channel comparisons vs one-Short days: 2/day = 1.29x per video (124 channels, 62.1% favored 2/day); 3/day = 1.02x (29 channels); 4+/day = 1.00x (21 channels). Combined daily views: 2.58x (2/day), 3.06x (3/day), 4.84x (4+/day). Author states it cannot test whether a second upload changes distribution of the first, did not test spacing, and is associational; author is the vendor's CEO. — [OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/how-many-youtube-shorts-per-day)
- [ANECDOTAL/PRACTITIONER — Dec 5, 2025] Retention strategist Mario Joos (works with MrBeast, Stokes Twins) reported that since mid-September 2025, Shorts older than ~28–30 days get far fewer impressions on large channels (100M–1B monthly views); creator Tim Chesney said evergreen Shorts "tanked" in September. Joos/Chesney believe this pushes creators toward more volume over quality. YouTube has not confirmed any change; some commenters saw no drop. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm-may-now-favor-fresh-over-evergreen/562661/)
- [VENDOR/BLOG — Feb 17, 2026] AIR Media-Tech lists over-posting risks: audience fatigue, the algorithm seeing a "repetitive premise" and no longer showing the content to new audiences, and quality dilution lowering retention. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/how-often-should-you-post-shorts-to-grow-faster)
- [VENDOR/BLOG — unverified] FlowShorts claims 10+ Shorts in a single day "can trigger YouTube's spam detection and reduce visibility"; no evidence given. Other blogs claim same-day uploads split the audience; one cites 40–60% same-day viewer overlap from unnamed "internal data" (unverifiable). — [FlowShorts](https://flowshorts.app/blog/how-many-shorts-per-day); [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/how-often-to-post-youtube-shorts)
- [VENDOR/BLOG] ClipSpeedAI (sells clipping tool) warns that past some point extra clips "dilute the account" and the recommender may read it as low-signal. — [ClipSpeedAI GTA 6 page](https://clipspeed.ai/gta6/how-many-gta-6-shorts-per-day.html)

### Inferences
- Evidence points to "each Short is judged on its own": more uploads mostly add total views without lifting or clearly lowering per-video views, up to ~4/day in the OverseerOS data. Beyond 4/day there is no published measurement (the 4+ bucket is small, 21 channels).
- The real "hurt" mechanisms are indirect: (a) lower average quality/retention if volume is forced, (b) notification pauses when >3 uploads land close together, (c) monetization risk from repetitive/template-like uploads under the inauthentic-content policy.
- If the ~30-day decay theory is right, a steady daily flow is more valuable than bursts.

### Gaps
- No controlled or official data on cannibalization between same-day Shorts, or on effects above ~4–5 Shorts/day per channel.
- No data on optimal spacing (hours between uploads) from any rigorous source; spacing advice (e.g., "a couple hours apart") is vendor editorial.

## 3. Hard limits: daily upload caps and YouTube Data API quota

### Takeaway
YouTube publishes no numeric daily upload limit; Help says uploads (videos and Shorts) have a "limited daily limit" for basic accounts and a "higher daily limit" for advanced-feature accounts (channel history or ID/video verification). The often-quoted "15 per day" is not official. Via the API, as of mid-2026 `videos.insert` has its own quota bucket defaulting to 100 calls/day per Google Cloud project (1 unit per call) — up from roughly 6 uploads/day before Dec 2025.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL — current] "There's a limit to how many times you can use certain features each day." Uploading videos and Shorts is listed as "Limited daily limit" without verification and "Higher daily limit" at the advanced tier. Intermediate features require phone verification; advanced features come from channel history or ID/video verification. Advanced users who hit a limit must wait 24 hours. No numbers are given. — [YouTube Help: feature access](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437?hl=en)
- [VENDOR/BLOG] The commonly cited "15 videos" figure is described by third parties as the YouTube Studio batch-selection limit (videos per upload batch), not an official daily cap; sources conflict. — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/youtube-daily-upload-limit/); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/youtube-upload-limits-complete-guide)
- [OFFICIAL — API revision history] Dec 4, 2025: docs updated to "reflect a change in the quota cost of a video upload from approximately 1600 units to approximately 100 units." June 1, 2026: "API calls to the `videos.insert` and `search.list` methods will be charged to their own respective quota buckets." — [YouTube Data API revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/revision_history)
- [OFFICIAL — current quota calculator] `videos.insert`: "100 quota per day. Each call costs 1 quota"; "The `search.list` and `videos.insert` methods have their own quota buckets"; all other methods share the default 10,000 units/day; quotas reset at midnight Pacific Time. (Note: an auto-generated page summary in search results still mentioned "1600", which conflicts with the page body; the body/table is the current value.) — [YouTube Data API: Quota calculator](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/determine_quota_cost)
- [VENDOR] Third-party API guides confirm the pre-Dec-2025 cost of ~1,600 units per upload (≈6 uploads/day on the default 10,000-unit quota). — [Activepieces](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/youtube-api-quota-exceeded-how-to-fix-and-avoid-it); [bundle.social](https://bundle.social/blog/youtube-api-quota-exceeded-limits-fixes)

### Inferences
- With default API quota, ~100 uploads/day per Google Cloud project is the ceiling (as of the current calculator). The quota is per project, so all channels posted through the same project share those 100 calls; at 10–20 clips/day per channel this is enough for roughly 5–10 channels at full volume before needing a quota extension. Thumbnail/metadata calls draw from the separate 10,000-unit pool.
- The account-side "limited daily limit" for unverified channels is undocumented; verifying the channel (phone → intermediate; ID/history → advanced) is the documented way to raise it.

### Gaps
- No official number for the daily upload limit per tier, and no confirmation whether Shorts share the same cap as long-form (the Help page lists "videos and Shorts" together, implying a shared limit).
- Whether API-uploaded Shorts are treated differently in distribution: no source found.

## 4. What do third-party data studies say about Shorts frequency vs. growth?

### Takeaway
Large 2026 datasets show more uploads correlate with faster growth (vidIQ, 10.2M channels, but mixing Shorts and long-form) and that extra Shorts per day add total views roughly linearly without improving per-video views (OverseerOS). No study isolates Shorts-only channels at 10+ uploads/day; the "10+/day = 2x views" claim (AIR Media-Tech) has no methodology. Buffer/Hootsuite/Metricool/Sprout/Socialinsider Shorts-frequency data were not found.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA — vidIQ, July 8, 2026; 10,210,278 channels with ≥1,000 subs; uploads Apr 2025–Mar 2026; counts long-form + Shorts together] Median monthly view growth by uploads/month: <1 = 0.53%; 1–3 = 0.89%; 4–7 = 1.32%; 8–11 = 1.70%; 12+ = 2.18%. Median monthly subscriber growth: 0.00%, 0.10%, 0.39%, 0.63%, 0.91%. 12+/month channels had 4.1x the view-growth rate of <1/month. "This study shows correlation, not causation." No plateau within tiers, but nothing measured above "12+/month" and Shorts not isolated. — [vidIQ upload frequency study](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-upload-frequency-study/)
- [DATA — vidIQ, July 28, 2026; channels created Jan–Jun 2025; 367,243 Shorts channels vs 137,355 long-form with ≥10 uploads in first 90 days] Median 30-day views per upload for Shorts: 1st = 1,362; 5th = 1,275; 10th = 1,344 (flat/slight decline) vs long-form 180 → 272 (+51%). Best of first 10 Shorts: median 8,832 views. 86.5% of Shorts channels had a best-of-10 ≥1,000 views; 48.0% ≥10,000. Per individual Short: 56.6% reached 1,000 views, 20.9% reached 10,000. — [vidIQ First 10 Videos study](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/)
- [DATA/VENDOR — OverseerOS, Oct 29, 2026, 16,815 Shorts] Per-video medians flat across 1–4+ Shorts/day; combined daily views 2.58x (2/day), 3.06x (3/day), 4.84x (4+/day) vs 1/day. — [OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/how-many-youtube-shorts-per-day)
- [VENDOR — AIR Media-Tech, updated Feb 17, 2026] "In 2025, our experts noticed that posting 10+ Shorts per day gave x2 views" (no sample/method disclosed; not presented as a target). Suggested cadences: start at 1/day and increase; 3–5/week baseline; 1–2/day for Shorts-only creators; gaming: "daily works best, but it needs to have soul." Recommends testing 5 Shorts/week for 8 weeks tracking retention and views per impression. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/how-often-should-you-post-shorts-to-grow-faster)
- [OLDER/NOT FOUND] Buffer's YouTube Shorts algorithm article (Sep 2023) contains no posting-frequency data. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)

### Inferences
- The data supports "more Shorts = more total views" up to at least ~4/day, with per-video performance roughly stable — so volume pays off only if each extra clip meets the same quality bar.
- vidIQ's new-channel data suggests Shorts get initial distribution (~1,300 median views per upload) from the first upload, but do not "warm up" a channel over the first 10 uploads — each Short stands alone, consistent with the "no cannibalization, no compounding" reading.

### Gaps
- No Shorts-only study measuring channels at 10–20 Shorts/day.
- No data found from TubeBuddy, Hootsuite, Metricool, Sprout Social or Socialinsider specifically on Shorts frequency (searches returned none).
- No gaming-niche or GTA-specific dataset; no PT-BR market data.

## 5. What do creators and forums say (anecdotal)?

### Takeaway
Reddit threads could not be retrieved directly; secondhand and blog consensus is 1–3 Shorts/day for most channels, with clip-tool vendors pushing 3–10+/day for clip channels. Brazilian Portuguese sources found were translated vendor blogs echoing 1–3/day. All of this is opinion, not measured.

### Cited Findings
- [VENDOR/BLOG] Common consensus range is 1–3 Shorts/day; one guide says the biggest mistake is starting at 3/day and burning out in three weeks. — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/how-often-to-post-youtube-shorts); [FlowShorts](https://flowshorts.app/blog/how-many-shorts-per-day)
- [VENDOR/BLOG] AutoClip: channels posting 1–2 Shorts/day consistently outperform channels that post 10/day for a week then go silent (no data shown). — [AutoClip](https://autoclip.dev/blog/how-many-clips-to-post-per-day)
- [VENDOR — ClipSpeedAI, July 9, 2026, GTA 6-specific, explicitly "editorial guidance"] Recommends 3–5/day in weeks 1–3, 5–8/day in month 1, scaling to 8–10+/day, floor of 3/day; space posts "a couple hours apart"; queue 1–2 days ahead; cross-post the same verticals to TikTok/Reels. Vendor sells the clipping tool; no data. — [ClipSpeedAI](https://clipspeed.ai/gta6/how-many-gta-6-shorts-per-day.html)
- [VENDOR — PT-BR] Brazilian Portuguese guides: start with 1–2 Shorts/day or 4–5/week; "most creators see good results posting 1–3 Shorts per day." — [Predis.ai (PT)](https://predis.ai/pt/resources/how-many-youtube-shorts-should-i-post/); [AIR Media-Tech (PT)](https://air.io/pt/youtube-hacks/how-often-should-you-post-shorts-to-grow-faster)
- [ANECDOTAL, secondhand] Reddit users reportedly suggest as few as 2–3 Shorts/week can work because quality beats quantity (secondhand blog summary, not a direct thread). — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/p/how-often-to-post-youtube-shorts)

### Inferences

- No Brazilian creator (YouTube/blog) with measured results for "canal de cortes" posting frequency was found.

## 6. New vs. established channels

### Takeaway
No official or rigorous source splits Shorts frequency advice by channel age. Editorial advice consistently says new channels should start at ~1–5/day and scale only as winners appear; data shows new channels' Shorts get roughly the same median distribution from the first upload (~1.3k views), so volume mainly buys more "lottery tickets."

### Cited Findings
- [DATA — vidIQ, 2026] New channels (created H1 2025): median Shorts views ~1,300–1,360 per upload across uploads 1–10, no upward trend; 48% of Shorts channels had at least one of their first 10 Shorts reach 10,000 views. — [vidIQ First 10 Videos study](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/)
- [DATA — vidIQ, 2026] Upload-frequency growth correlation measured only on channels with ≥1,000 subscribers; no age/size split. — [vidIQ upload frequency study](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-upload-frequency-study/)
- [VENDOR] New GTA 6 clip channels: 3–5/day for weeks 1–3 to learn which hooks work; established: 8–10+/day. — [ClipSpeedAI](https://clipspeed.ai/gta6/how-many-gta-6-shorts-per-day.html)
- [VENDOR] Start with 1 Short/day and increase gradually; test 5/week for 8 weeks. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/how-often-should-you-post-shorts-to-grow-faster)
- [OFFICIAL] New (unverified) channels have a "limited daily limit" on uploads; higher limits require channel history or ID verification. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437?hl=en)
- [DATA/VENDOR] OverseerOS's beginner vs established guidance is editorial, not a study finding. — [OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/how-many-youtube-shorts-per-day)

### Inferences
- For a new PT-BR GTA 6 clips channel, a defensible starting range from the evidence is ~3–5 Shorts/day per channel, spaced several hours apart (to avoid the >3-in-a-short-period notification pause), scaling toward the tool's 10–20/day only if per-Short retention/views hold. Spreading the 10–20 daily clips across several channels/platforms fits the evidence better than dumping them all on one new channel.
- A new channel may hit the undocumented "limited daily limit" before 10–20/day; phone and ID verification are the documented way to lift it.

### Gaps
- No data comparing outcomes of new channels posting 1/day vs 5/day vs 10+/day.
- No GTA 6-specific or PT-BR gaming-specific evidence; the GTA 6 release window effect on Shorts demand is unmeasured.
